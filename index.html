Why the long table

Some of the best conversations happen at the table

Jesus did some of His most important talking around a table. He ate with friends, with doubters and with people nobody else would sit with. That's the idea behind Long Table Media: real conversations about faith and life, where everyone gets a seat and the Bible gets the last word.
