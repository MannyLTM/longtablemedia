The Internet Is Laughing at Susan Dell. Here's What I Found After the Camera Stopped

The whole internet is roasting Susan Dell's face. Meanwhile a former UFC fighter just got shot in Congo. I think we've got our priorities backwards.

By Manny · October 13, 2026 · 4 min read

Hey! Every time I got on the internet this week, I kept seeing the same name: Susan Dell, the wife of Michael Dell (yes, the Dell computers guy). She showed up at the White House to announce a huge donation to help kids start investment accounts. And what did the internet talk about? Her face. People called her a mask, a reptile, an alien. So I reacted to Elijah Zielke's breakdown of it, and after the camera stopped, I couldn't shake one thought: we've got everything backwards.

We don't know her story

People change. Life happens. You get sick, you go through trauma, you get older, you make a decision you regret. (Trust me, if I showed you a picture of me when I was younger, you'd say I look way better now.) The point is, none of us know what she's been through. And God doesn't look at people the way the internet does.

People judge by outward appearance, but the LORD looks at the heart.1 Samuel 16:7 (NLT)

Her husband said it best: the internet has made a lot of people way too comfortable disrespecting someone they don't know. Imagine somebody doing that to your wife or your husband.

Would you be ready?

While everyone was laughing, former UFC fighter Tim Kennedy was shot three times in Congo, and others on his team didn't make it. He's a believer. So the real question isn't what Susan Dell looks like. It's this: if that were you, would you be ready to meet God? Not because of anything we've done, but because of what Jesus did for us.

If you openly declare that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.Romans 10:9 (NLT)

The Chipotle thing

The video also brought up Chipotle's Halloween post that looked like a Ouija board. Honestly? Chipotle never claimed to be a Christian company. They're not Chick-fil-A. I'm not going to panic about a burrito chain. But the bigger issue is real: more and more people are playing with things like witchcraft and the occult without knowing what they're opening the door to. We don't need fear. We need discernment.

But you belong to God, my dear children. You have already won a victory over those people, because the Spirit who lives in you is greater than the spirit who lives in the world.1 John 4:4 (NLT)
My conclusion: It's too easy to type something cruel behind a screen. But if you wouldn't say it to someone's face at the park, don't say it online. That lady was trying to do something good for kids. Let's treat people the way we'd want to be treated, and spend our energy on the things that really matter.
Do to others whatever you would like them to do to you.Matthew 7:12 (NLT)
