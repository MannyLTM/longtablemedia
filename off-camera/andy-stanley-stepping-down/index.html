Andy Stanley Is Stepping Down After 31 Years. What I Found After the Camera Stopped

After 31 years at North Point, Andy Stanley says it's time. Here's my honest take on his legacy, and the bigger lesson for all of us.

By Manny · October 22, 2026 · 4 min read

Hey! After 31 years, Andy Stanley announced he's stepping down as senior pastor of North Point. He says he wants to be able to say yes to his grandkids without checking his schedule. Joel Thomas has been named as his successor, with members voting on November 1. I reacted to the announcement, and after the camera stopped, here's where I landed.

Give credit where it's due

Thirty-one years is a long time to faithfully lead a church. North Point has reached a lot of people who never would have walked into a church building. And wanting more time with your family is a good, godly reason. Family is ministry too.

Remember your leaders who taught you the word of God. Think of all the good that has come from their lives, and follow the example of their faith.Hebrews 13:7 (NLT)

But we can't ignore the controversies

I have to be honest. Andy Stanley has said things that made a lot of believers uneasy, like telling Christians to "unhitch" their faith from the Old Testament, and the Unconditional conference. For me, the whole Bible is God's Word, Old and New. A pastor's job is to preach all of it, not just the parts that are easy to hear.

All Scripture is inspired by God and is useful to teach us what is true and to make us realize what is wrong in our lives. It corrects us when we are wrong and teaches us to do what is right.2 Timothy 3:16 (NLT)

The bigger lesson

Pastors are human. They retire, they move on, and sometimes they fall. If your faith is built on a pastor, it'll shake when they leave. If it's built on Jesus and His Word, it won't. Let's pray for North Point and for Joel Thomas as he steps in.

My conclusion: Honor the years, be honest about the teaching, and build your faith on the One who never steps down.
Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever.Hebrews 13:8 (NLT)
