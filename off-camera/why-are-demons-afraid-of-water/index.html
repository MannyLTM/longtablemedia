Are Demons Really Afraid of Water? What I Found After the Camera Stopped

A pastor says demons hate water because they're the spirits of the giants who drowned in the flood. Here's where I landed.

By Manny · October 18, 2026 · 4 min read

Hey! I keep hearing this one: demons are afraid of water. On The Deep End with Taylor Welch, Pastor Fred K. Price Jr. said demons aren't fallen angels at all. He said they're the spirits of the giants who died in Noah's flood, and that's why they hate water. I had to react. After the camera stopped, here's where I landed.

The giants theory

The idea comes mostly from the Book of Enoch: angels called Watchers had children with human women, those children were giants, and when the flood destroyed their bodies, their spirits were left roaming the earth. It's a fascinating story, and I'll be honest, I went back and forth on it. But Enoch isn't in our Bible, and Scripture never says demons are dead giants. Revelation 12:9 says Satan was thrown down "with all his angels." That's the clearest picture we get.

Are they really afraid of water?

I don't think so. Water is just an element. If demons are spiritual beings, why would they fear something physical? Jesus does say evil spirits wander through dry places looking for rest, but that's about having no home, not being scared of a puddle.

When an evil spirit leaves a person, it goes into the desert, searching for rest but finding none.Matthew 12:43 (NLT)

What they ARE afraid of

Here's what the Bible does show: demons are terrified of the bottomless pit. When Jesus confronted Legion, they begged Him not to send them there. That's a place of judgment, and if people are scared of going to jail, imagine being scared of that.

The demons kept begging Jesus not to send them into the bottomless pit.Luke 8:31 (NLT)
My conclusion: Fallen angels or giants, whatever these things are, they all have to bow to the name of Jesus. And if you're in the light, the darkness can't touch you.
...that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth.Philippians 2:10 (NLT)
