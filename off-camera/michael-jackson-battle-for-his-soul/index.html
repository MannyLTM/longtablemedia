Did Michael Jackson Accept Jesus? What I Found After the Camera Stopped

He tried to destroy Thriller to stay in his church. One week before he died, he was holding hands with gospel singers, praying “Lord, change my life.”

By Manny · October 28, 2026 · 4 min read

Hey! Confession: when I was a kid I loved Thriller. I'd try to do the dance in my room. But when that scary voice came on at the end, I was so scared I'd fast-forward the cassette every single time. What I never knew back then? Michael Jackson was scared of that video too. So when Whaddo You Meme?? put out a video on the battle for Michael Jackson's soul, I had to react. After the camera stopped, here's where I landed.

He tried to destroy Thriller

Michael was raised a Jehovah's Witness from age five. Even as the biggest star in the world, he went door to door in a disguise handing out magazines. When the elders called Thriller demonic and threatened to cast him out, he ordered the film destroyed, locked himself in a bathroom, and stopped eating. The warning at the start of the video is there because he was trying to be good enough to stay.

God saved you by his grace when you believed. And you can't take credit for this; it is a gift from God. Salvation is not a reward for the good things we have done, so none of us can boast about it.Ephesians 2:8–9 (NLT)

Everybody pulled at him

A rabbi, a psychic who hypnotized him, the Nation of Islam, his old church. Everyone wanted a piece of Michael. But when things got darkest, he called a Christian producer named Rodney Jerkins and said, “I need you to pray for me.” The first thing he said when he walked in was, “I sense a sweet spirit in this place.” Ordinary believers who live it out, without judging, are who people call in the storm.

Did he accept Jesus?

One week before he died, Andraé and Sandra Crouch prayed with him: “Lord, change Michael's life.” And Michael prayed it back. Did he truly give his life to Jesus? Only God knows his heart, and I'm not going to pretend I do. But I do know grace can reach anyone, even at the very end.

And Jesus replied, “I assure you, today you will be with me in paradise.”Luke 23:43 (NLT)
My conclusion: Michael once said, “I wanted to be loved… I never really felt loved.” A lot of us know what it's like to perform for love. Jesus never asked Michael to be good enough. He just said come.
However, those the Father has given me will come to me, and I will never reject them.John 6:37 (NLT)
