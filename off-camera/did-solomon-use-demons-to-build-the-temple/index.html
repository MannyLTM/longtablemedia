Did King Solomon Really Use Demons to Build the Temple? What I Found After the Camera Stopped

A legend says Solomon trapped demons with a magic ring and made them build God's Temple. Here's what the Bible actually says.

By Manny · October 23, 2026 · 4 min read

Hey! Have you heard this one? King Solomon, the wisest man who ever lived, supposedly built God's Temple using demons, and trapped them with a magic ring. On Camp Gagnon, Dr. Justin Sledge walked through the legends: the Testament of Solomon, a magic worm called the Shamir, the demon Asmodeus, and an occult book called the Lesser Key of Solomon. I had to react. After the camera stopped, here's where I landed.

The Bible tells a different story

The demon stories come from later legends and the Talmud, not Scripture. The Bible actually tells us how the stones were cut, and it's not with worms or demons. It was workers and God's design.

The stones used in the construction of the Temple were finished at the quarry, so there was no sound of hammer, ax, or any other iron tool at the building site.1 Kings 6:7 (NLT)

Solomon's real downfall

Here's the scarier part. Solomon's problem wasn't that he controlled demons. It's that the wisest man alive slowly drifted away from God. That's the real warning for us.

In Solomon's old age, they turned his heart to worship other gods instead of being completely faithful to the LORD his God, as his father, David, had been.1 Kings 11:4 (NLT)

Someone greater than Solomon

Jesus didn't need a ring, a seal, or a spellbook. He cast out evil spirits with a word. Power over darkness isn't a ritual. It's Jesus.

That evening many demon-possessed people were brought to Jesus. He cast out the evil spirits with a simple command, and he healed all the sick.Matthew 8:16 (NLT)
My conclusion: The Lesser Key of Solomon is still used in the occult today. Don't mess with that stuff. If you play with fire, you're gonna get burned.
