The Bible Has a Ghost Story. Was It Really Samuel? What I Found After the Camera Stopped

King Saul went to a medium, and a dead prophet spoke. Was it really Samuel, or something darker? Here's where I landed.

By Manny · October 19, 2026 · 4 min read

Hey! Did you know the Bible has a ghost story? In 1 Samuel 28, King Saul is desperate. God has gone silent, so he disguises himself and goes to a medium in Endor and asks her to bring up the prophet Samuel. Wise Disciple made a great video on it, and I had to react. After the camera stopped, here's where I landed.

Why did the medium scream?

This is the part that gets me. The woman supposedly talks to the dead for a living, but when Samuel actually shows up, she screams. Maybe she had been faking it all along, and this time something real happened that she didn't expect. The Bible doesn't tell us exactly why, but her reaction says a lot.

Was it really Samuel?

Some people say it was a demon pretending to be Samuel. But the text calls him Samuel, and what he says comes true: Saul and his sons die the next day. I think God allowed it for a specific purpose, to deliver judgment. That doesn't mean God approved of what Saul did. In fact, it's listed as one of the reasons Saul died.

So Saul died because he was unfaithful to the LORD. He failed to obey the LORD's command, and he even consulted a medium for guidance.1 Chronicles 10:13 (NLT)

So are ghosts real?

Honestly, I'm skeptical. The Bible never teaches that the dead wander around haunting houses. If people are seeing something, I believe it's more likely demonic, something trying to confuse people. And God is very clear about trying to contact the dead.

And do not let your people practice fortune-telling, or use sorcery, or interpret omens, or engage in witchcraft, or cast spells, or function as mediums or psychics, or call forth the spirits of the dead.Deuteronomy 18:10–11 (NLT)
My conclusion: Don't mess with that stuff. No mediums, no Ouija boards, no hanging out in "haunted" places. If you play with fire, you're gonna get burned.
