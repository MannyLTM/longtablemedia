Shawn Ryan's 5th Exorcism: What I Found After the Camera Stopped

Shawn Ryan says he's been through five exorcisms. Here's what oppression really means, what Scripture says about it, and why it's making his faith stronger.

By Manny · October 10, 2026 · 4 min read

Hey! In my first Shawn Ryan video, he had been through four exorcisms. Now he says it's five. In this video I reacted to his interview with Steven Bartlett on The Diary of a CEO, where he opened up about what it's actually like, and what it's doing to his wife and kids.

It's not like the movies

Shawn says it's nothing like The Exorcist. No crawling up walls. It's a series of prayers, some in Latin and some in English, while the priests watch for reactions to figure out what they're dealing with.

Possession vs. oppression

This was the most helpful part. Shawn explained there are two kinds:

  • Possession: you're not in control of your mind or your body.
  • Oppression: you're still in control, but things start coming against you: your money, your health, your family, your faith.

His priests say his case is oppression. And that's something every Christian can relate to on some level, because the Bible is clear that the fight is real:

For we are not fighting against flesh-and-blood enemies, but against evil rulers and authorities of the unseen world, against mighty powers in this dark world, and against evil spirits in the heavenly places.Ephesians 6:12 (NLT)

What it's doing to his family

Shawn said: imagine coming home to your wife and kids and saying, "I just got done with my fifth exorcism today." He wonders what his wife thinks when she goes to bed next to him. That hit me. And he said he's heard from SEALs and Delta guys who told him, "I thought I was the only one."

The enemy meant it for harm

Here's what stood out most. Shawn said it's scary, but it gets less scary, and it's strengthening his faith and his wife's faith in Christ. That's what God does. He takes what the enemy meant for harm and uses it for good.

And we know that God causes everything to work together for the good of those who love God and are called according to his purpose for them.Romans 8:28 (NLT)

Your soul is worth more

Later in the interview, Shawn shared that a longtime advertiser dropped him because they don't advertise on faith-based content. He's doing the Bible series anyway, because, in his words, he can't take that money with him.

And what do you benefit if you gain the whole world but lose your own soul? Is anything worth more than your soul?Matthew 16:26 (NLT)
My conclusion: Whatever you think about exorcisms, Shawn is being honest about a real spiritual battle, and it's pushing him toward Jesus, not away. If you're a believer going through something heavy, you're not crazy and you're not alone. Resist the enemy, stay close to Jesus, and keep praying for Shawn and his family.
So humble yourselves before God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.James 4:7 (NLT)
