Is the Antichrist Already Here? What I Found After the Camera Stopped

Sam Shamoun says the spirit of antichrist is already at work. Here's what the Bible actually says about the antichrist, and where I land as a Protestant.

By Manny · October 14, 2026 · 4 min read

Hey! If I told you the Antichrist is already here, would you believe it? That's basically what Sam Shamoun, a Christian apologist, is saying. And he doesn't hold back. He goes after false teachers, the Reformers, and even how we think about salvation. As a Protestant, some of it hit close to home. So after I stopped recording, I went back to the Scriptures.

Is the Antichrist already here?

Here's the thing a lot of people miss: the Bible talks about the Antichrist who is coming, but it also says there are many antichrists already.

Dear children, the last hour is here. You have heard that the Antichrist is coming, and already many such antichrists have appeared. From this we know that the last hour has come.1 John 2:18 (NLT)

So in one sense, Sam is right. The spirit of antichrist has been at work since the days of the apostles. And John tells us exactly what it looks like:

I say this because many deceivers have gone out into the world. They deny that Jesus Christ came in a real body. Such a person is a deceiver and an antichrist.2 John 1:7 (NLT)

The test isn't a scary symbol or a world leader. It's what someone says about Jesus. Anything that denies who He really is, God who came in the flesh, carries that spirit.

Watch out for smooth talk

Sam also pointed to Paul's warning about people who cause division and teach things contrary to what we were taught:

And now I make one more appeal, my dear brothers and sisters. Watch out for people who cause divisions and upset people's faith by teaching things contrary to what you have been taught. Stay away from them.Romans 16:17 (NLT)

That one convicted me a little. It made me ask: when people look at my life, am I really showing Jesus magnified? Or am I just doing whatever I want? The spirit of antichrist isn't only "out there." We have to check our own hearts too.

The Reformers and salvation

This is where it got spicy. Sam goes after the Reformers, and he challenges the idea that once you're saved, you can live however you want. Christians have debated this for centuries, so I won't pretend it's settled in one blog post. But here's something every believer can agree on: real faith shows up in how we live.

So you see, faith by itself isn't enough. Unless it produces good deeds, it is dead and useless.James 2:17 (NLT)

And the good news is that our security doesn't rest on how strong our grip is. It rests on Jesus.

I give them eternal life, and they will never perish. No one can snatch them away from me.John 10:28 (NLT)

Not every path leads to God

Sam also made the point that not all paths lead to God, and I shared a story from Facebook in the video that brought this home for me. A lot of people today want every road to end in the same place. But I stand on this: I believe Jesus is the way, the one and only way.

Jesus told him, "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one can come to the Father except through me."John 14:6 (NLT)
My conclusion: Is the Antichrist already here? The spirit of antichrist is, and it always shows up as a lie about Jesus. You don't need to panic about the end times. You need to know Jesus so well that you can spot a counterfeit, and live in a way that magnifies Him.
