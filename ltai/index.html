How it works

Every episode follows the same simple plan.

1

We watch

A trending clip about God, the Bible, culture or AI. The stuff people are already arguing about.

2

We react

Honest first thoughts. Sometimes I agree, sometimes I push back, sometimes I say "I don't get it" out loud.

3

We open the Word

Then we check it against Scripture, because the Bible gets the last word. No hype, no hate.
